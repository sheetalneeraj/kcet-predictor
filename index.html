About the Data

📋

Official KEA PDFs

Round-wise cutoff PDFs published at cetonline.karnataka.gov.in after each counselling session for 2020–2024.

📰

Verified Portals

Cross-referenced with Careers360, Shiksha, CollegeDunia, and Sakshi Education for accuracy.

🔢

50:50 Formula

Rank computed as: (KCET/180×90) + (PUC/300×90) = Combined score out of 180. Higher = better rank.

🔄
